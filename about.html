About Het Palett

A small, independent practice where personal connection comes first.

Gerda Vleugel

Gerda Vleugel founded Het Palett in 2005 after nearly two decades working in institutional mental healthcare. She saw that larger organisations increasingly focused on efficiency targets rather than the personal relationship with clients.

For Gerda, the personal connection between therapist and client is everything. That's why she started her own practice — to offer the kind of care she believes in: personal, flexible and human.

With over 30 years of experience, Gerda has worked as a therapist, coach, mediator and advisor. She brings this broad experience to every session, adapting her approach to what each individual student needs.

Why Het Palett?

Het Palett means "The Palette" in Dutch — reflecting the colourful variety of people and approaches that make up our practice. Just as every colour on a palette contributes to a complete picture, every person brings their own unique story.

  • Personal — You're not a number. We take time to truly understand your situation.
  • Flexible — We schedule around your classes and commitments.
  • Affordable — Student-friendly rates available.
  • Central — Located in the centre of Groningen, easy to reach by bike or bus.
  • No waiting lists — We can usually see you within a week.

Our approach

We believe in building a relationship of trust and openness. In our sessions, you set the pace. There's no judgement, no pressure — just a safe space to explore what's on your mind.

We combine talk therapy with practical approaches like coaching and light therapy, depending on what works best for you.
