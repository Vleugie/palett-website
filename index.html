We understand student life

Moving to a new country is exciting, but it can also be incredibly challenging. Being far from family, adjusting to a new culture, dealing with academic pressure and dark winters can take a real toll on your well-being.

At Het Palett, we specialise in supporting international students. Gerda Vleugel has over 30 years of experience in mental healthcare and understands the unique challenges you face.

  • All sessions in English
  • Flexible scheduling around your classes
  • Affordable rates for students
  • Central location in Groningen

Common challenges we help with

  • Anxiety and panic attacks
  • Depression and low mood
  • Homesickness and loneliness
  • Academic stress and burnout
  • Seasonal affective disorder (SAD)
  • Relationship difficulties
  • Cultural adjustment
  • Self-esteem and identity

A personal approach

Het Palett is a small, independent practice where personal connection comes first. The relationship between you and your therapist is the foundation for meaningful progress.

We create a safe, open and honest environment built on mutual trust. No waiting lists, no bureaucracy — just real support when you need it.
