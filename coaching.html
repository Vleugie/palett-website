Coaching

Build confidence, find direction and unlock your potential.

Discover your strengths

Everyone has qualities and talents, but sometimes it's hard to see them clearly — especially when you're under pressure, in a new environment, or questioning your path.

Coaching at Het Palett helps you reconnect with your strengths, build confidence and create a clear direction for your studies and personal life.

Coaching can help with

  • Finding motivation and direction
  • Building self-confidence
  • Managing academic pressure
  • Making important life decisions
  • Developing better study habits
  • Improving communication skills
  • Setting and achieving personal goals

Coaching vs. therapy

Coaching is forward-looking and goal-oriented. It's about where you want to go, not where you've been. If you're not struggling with mental health issues but want to grow, improve or make changes, coaching is the right fit.

Not sure which is right for you? We'll figure it out together in your first consultation.

Practical & results-driven

Coaching sessions are practical and focused. We work with concrete goals and actionable steps. Most students see results within 3-5 sessions.
